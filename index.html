About Me

Dr. Xiaole Yin is currently a Research Fellow in Medicine at Brigham and Women’s Hospital, Harvard Medical School in Boston, United States. She obtained her PhD from The University of Hong Kong, supervised by Chair Professor Tong Zhang and earned her master’s and bachelor’s degrees from Shandong University supervised by Professor Jian Zhang. Dr. Yin's research focuses on data-driven exploration of the intricate interactions among the microbiome, humans, and the environment. By developing advanced computational tools and integrating deep learning techniques, her work deciphers the evolution and dissemination of antibiotic resistance genes (ARGs) and pathogens through large-scale metagenomic analyses and predictive modeling. Additionally, she applies cutting-edge bioinformatics to study the role of the microbiome in human gastroenterology, uncovering novel pathogenic mechanisms to guide disease diagnosis and intervention.

She has led the development of several acclaimed bioinformatics tools, such as ARGs-OAP (Antibiotic Resistance Genes - Online Analysis Platform, with 46,506 visits as of Jan 5th, 2025), Sargfam (Hidden Markov Models of ARG genotypes), ARGs-MST, ICEcream, ARGbert, ResistanceWWTP and others.

Research Interests

My research focuses on computational biology and experimental validatiaon to understand the complexities of the human microbiome, investigating the mechanisms of antibiotic resistance in environmental settings, and developing novel multi-omics approaches to unravel biological systems. My work aims to contribute to the development of new diagnostic and therapeutic strategies by leveraging large-scale biological datasets and advanced computational methodologies.
