Xiaole Yin, Ph.D.

Principal Investigator

Dr. Xiaole Yin leads this research group, focusing on cutting-edge bioinformatics research. Her work encompasses the study of the human and environmental microbiome, antibiotic resistance, and the application of multi-omics and machine learning techniques to complex biological datasets. Dr. Yin is dedicated to fostering a collaborative and innovative research environment and is actively seeking passionate PhD students to join the team and contribute to impactful scientific discoveries. Her detailed biography and research interests can be found on the Home page.
